EKOS — Enterprise Knowledge Operating System

Token utility, earned — not promised.

EKOS aims to become the operating system for enterprise knowledge. The EKOS token is designed to coordinate, reward, and sustain the people who build that infrastructure — its relevance is meant to grow the same way the platform's usefulness grows: as a consequence of adoption, never as a claim about future price.

This document deliberately avoids describing the token in terms of future price. Instead it describes what the token is for at each stage of the platform — utility that compounds with real usage, the same way the Canonical Knowledge Model itself compounds with every source system connected.

§ 02 / phases 1–3
Contributors first

Community, then a knowledge network, then a marketplace to sell into.

  • Phase 1 — CommunityOpen source, docs, discussions. Token rewards bug reports, docs, parsers, connectors, tutorials, moderation. Contribute knowledge → earn EKOS.
  • Phase 2 — Knowledge NetworkUsers extend recovery coverage — SQL, Pentaho, dbt, metadata. Token funds community grants, contributor rewards, bounties, plugin development.
  • Phase 3 — Plugin MarketplaceAny developer publishes a parser, connector, prompt pack, or agent, attributed by name. Free or premium; premium is priced and paid in EKOS, to the author.
§ 03 / phases 4–6
Where enterprises start paying

Utility that only matters once a company is already on the platform.

  • Phase 4 — Enterprise AI PlatformA company connects GitHub, Postgres, Confluence, Databricks, Synapse, Pentaho; EKOS compiles a Knowledge Graph AI can query. AI credits, premium agents, cloud processing, and knowledge packs can be paid in EKOS alongside fiat.
  • Phase 5 — AI Agent MarketplacePublished, authored agents — Pentaho Migration Agent, SQL Optimizer, Databricks Reviewer, Data Lineage Explorer. The author earns EKOS when others use the agent.
  • Phase 6 — Knowledge MarketplaceCompanies license curated Knowledge Packs for legacy ecosystems — SAP, Oracle EBS, Snowflake, Fabric, dbt — instead of rebuilding that recovery coverage themselves. Paid in EKOS.
§ 04 / phases 7–9
Governance without letting a large wallet buy influence

Reputation gates what a token balance alone cannot.

  • Phase 7 — GovernanceScoped to product decisions, not a general DAO — e.g. which legacy-format parser to build next (Talend, SSIS, ADF, Fabric). Voting weight tied to EKOS.
  • Phase 8 — ReputationReputation is tracked separately from token holdings. Becoming a maintainer requires reputation and a modest EKOS stake — large holdings alone don't confer influence without a track record of contribution.
  • Phase 9 — Enterprise MarketplaceA company posts a need ("Pentaho migration"); the community responds; the company picks a winner, paid in EKOS.
§ 05 / the mechanism
Not proof of work. Not proof of stake.

Proof of Knowledge.

Anyone can contribute knowledge — a parser, a lineage mapping, documentation, a schema mapping, an ontology, an agent, a connector. The more useful the contribution, the more EKOS it earns. The token is the distribution mechanism for value moving between the people who create knowledge and the people who use it — not a bet on the loop happening.
